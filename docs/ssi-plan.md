# SSI growth plan

LinkedIn's **Social Selling Index (SSI)** scores you from 0 to 100, updated daily, in four parts of 25 points each.
It also ranks you against your industry and your network. Check it at <https://www.linkedin.com/sales/ssi>.

## The four parts and what moves each one

### 1. Establish your professional brand (0–25)
What counts: a complete, keyword-rich profile; posting and publishing; and people engaging with what you post (views,
followers, likes, comments, shares).

- [ ] Finish every item in `docs/profile-and-page.md`: headline, About, Featured, banner, custom URL, Services, skills
- [ ] Turn on creator mode features (the Follow button) and add 5 topics
- [ ] Keep posting **daily from the founder profile** (the AM content calendar) and reshare every company post with your own line
- [ ] Publish **one LinkedIn article or newsletter edition a month** ("The CX Ops Brief"), built from the best posts of the month. Articles count as publishing.
- [ ] Ask 5–10 past colleagues and clients for skill endorsements and 2–3 recommendations

### 2. Find the right people (0–25)
What counts: using search (especially Sales Navigator) to find the right decision-makers, viewing their profiles,
saving leads, and getting profile views from your target audience.

- [ ] Build 3 **saved searches** in Sales Navigator, one per segment:
  - Logistics: Owner / Founder / CEO / COO / Head of Ops / Head of Customer Service, couriers, freight, 3PL, last-mile, 10–500 staff, US/CA/UK/AU
  - Robotics & hardware: the same roles at consumer robotics, hardware, 3D printing and drone companies
  - DTC e-commerce: Founder / Head of CX / Head of Ecommerce at apparel, beauty and home brands on Shopify
- [ ] **Save 10 leads a day** from those searches, and save the accounts (Pudu, Dyno Global, Phantom Couriers...)
- [ ] View the profiles of everyone who viewed yours, and of the people who comment on target accounts' posts
- [ ] Use the Sales Navigator lead alerts (job changes, posts) as your daily "who to engage" list

### 3. Engage with insights (0–25)
What counts: sharing useful content and engaging with your network's content (comments, reactions, shares), plus
replies to your messages and InMails.

- [ ] **10 thoughtful comments a day** (2+ sentences each) on posts from target-segment people. The comment playbook is in our chat history: add a real point, no pitching.
- [ ] Reply to every comment on your own posts within the first hour
- [ ] End every post with a question, because comments on your posts feed this part
- [ ] Keep InMails short and personal (like the Paul and Pudu drafts) so the reply rate stays high. Unanswered InMails hurt this part.
- [ ] Join 2–3 active groups (e.g. last-mile/courier operators, Shopify/DTC founders, CX leaders) and comment there weekly

### 4. Build relationships (0–25)
What counts: growing connections with decision-makers (Director level and up), acceptance rate on invites, and
relationships inside target accounts.

- [ ] **15 personalised invites a weekday** via `npm run queue`, aimed at Director+ titles in the 3 segments
- [ ] Keep acceptance above 30%: personalised notes and tight targeting. Withdraw invites still pending after 3 weeks.
- [ ] Connect with 2–3 people per target account (e.g. Pudu: CS head, ops lead, US sales), not just one
- [ ] Welcome every new connection (the welcome templates) and move warm replies into the deal tracker (`npm run deals`)

## Daily 30-minute routine

| Minutes | Task | SSI part |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Post today's founder post (and reshare yesterday's company post) | Brand, Insights |
| 5 | Reply to comments on your posts | Insights |
| 10 | 10 comments on target-segment posts (from Sales Nav alerts or the feed) | Insights, Find people |
| 5 | Save 10 leads from a saved search and view their profiles | Find people |
| 5 | Send the day's invites from the queue and welcome new connections | Relationships |

## How the content calendar supports SSI

- **Founder AM posts** count toward Brand. Pop-culture hooks and closing questions bring comments, which count toward Insights.
- **Company PM posts, reshared with a personal line,** count toward Brand and reach the page's followers.
- **Segment rotation** (logistics, robotics, DTC) means each saved-search audience regularly sees something written for them, so profile views come from the right people (Find people).
- **The monthly article** counts toward Brand and gives you something useful to send in follow-ups (Insights).
