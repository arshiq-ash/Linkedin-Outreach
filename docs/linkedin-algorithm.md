# How LinkedIn decides who sees our posts (working notes)

LinkedIn doesn't publish its ranking weights. These notes combine what third-party analyses broadly agree on with
what our own analytics show (`content/analytics/`). Treat any exact percentages you read elsewhere as estimates.

## How a post travels

1. **Quality check (first minutes):** spam, engagement bait and low-quality signals are filtered out.
2. **Test audience:** the post goes to a small sample of first-degree connections and followers.
3. **Signals collected:** how long people stop on it (dwell time), "…see more" clicks, substantive comments,
   replies from the author, saves and sends.
4. **Wider distribution:** if the test goes well, it's shown to more connections, then to **people outside our network
   whose behaviour shows interest in the topic**. This interest-based reach is how a post reaches people who aren't
   connected to us.
5. **Network ripple:** when someone comments, the post can appear in *their* connections' feeds too.

## What this means for us

| Lever | What we do |
|---|---|
| First line under 8 words that stops the scroll | Famous quote or bold claim first ("I DECLARE BANKRUPTCY!" got about 3x the reach of our other posts) |
| Dwell time | Concrete scenarios, short lines, a list people read through, a card that rewards a second look |
| Topic consistency | Always CX ops for DTC, logistics and robotics, so LinkedIn learns which interests to match us to |
| Substantive comments | End with an easy, specific question. Reply to every comment within the first hour. |
| Saves and sends | Checklists and frameworks people want to keep (BFCM dry run, Q4 calendar) |
| Network ripple | Leave 10 thoughtful comments a day on posts from ICP leaders, which brings our name to their audience |
| Profile clarity | Headline and About state who we help, so people who find us through topic reach understand us in 3 seconds |

## What to avoid

- Plain reposts (our lowest reach). Reshare with our own take instead.
- Hiring posts on the founder feed. They attract job seekers and blur our topic. Use the company page or LinkedIn Jobs.
- Engagement pods, "comment YES" bait without real value, and mass tagging. These are filtered as low quality.
- Links in the post body. Put them in the first comment.
- Generic, AI-sounding advice with no specifics. It gets low dwell time.

## Reaching people who don't need us yet

Most viewers won't need CX support today, and that's fine. Interest-based distribution puts us in front of founders
and ops leads who care about Q4, WISMO, returns and 24/7 coverage. Each post that lands builds familiarity, so when
their support breaks (peak season, a viral moment, a new market), we're the name they remember.
Measure progress through profile views from our target segments, follower growth and inbound DMs, not just likes.
