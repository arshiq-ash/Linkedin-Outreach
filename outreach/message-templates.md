# Outreach message templates

These are for **manual** sending. Personalise the first line every time: a post they wrote, a launch, a hire, a funding round.
Connection notes are limited to 200 characters. On a free account LinkedIn also caps how many notes you can send each
month. When you run out, send without a note and use message 1 after they accept.

## Connection notes (`npm run queue` fills these in automatically)

**Logistics**
> Hi {first}, {hook}. I run a CX ops team that supports delivery companies (90–100 calls/agent/day). Would love to connect.

**Robotics / hardware**
> Hi {first}, {hook}. We built a 15-person 24/7 pre + after-sales team for a robotics brand. Always keen to swap notes with hardware folks.

**DTC**
> Hi {first}, {hook}. I help DTC brands run WISMO/returns support 24/7. No pitch, just like connecting with good operators.

## After they accept

**Message 1: same day, no pitch**
> Thanks for connecting, {first}! Curious: at {company}, is support handled in-house right now, or is it still the founders + one heroic CX person? 😄

**Message 2: day 4, useful content (only if they replied or engaged)**
> Thought of you when I wrote this: {link to the most relevant post, e.g. the "No half measures" escalation checklist for logistics}. The step most teams skip is #3.

**Message 3: day 10, soft ask**
> Not sure if it's on your radar for Q4, but we usually start with a 2–4 week pilot on one channel (email or chat) so brands can judge the quality before committing. Happy to share how we did it for {closest case study}. Worth a 15-min chat?

**Stop after 3 messages.** If they haven't replied, engage with their posts instead. Don't keep sending messages.

## Rules

- Never paste the same message to more than one person without changing the first line.
- No links in the connection note.
- Reply within 2 hours during US business hours. The Pakistan evening overlaps nicely.
